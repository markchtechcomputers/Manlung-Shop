About Adict Manlung

Adict Manlung's music story began around 2018, while he was still in school. Lyrics were written in the same exercise books used for school subjects. After a difficult school chapter, he returned and completed Form Four.

After school he kept writing and recording, although many early tracks stayed unreleased. In 2022, My Gee became the first official release and opened a catalogue shaped by friendship, ambition, setbacks, survival and growth.

The journey moved between Eldoret and Nairobi. A later Nairobi chapter involved detention and court proceedings, followed by a return to Eldoret and a rebuild. The music continued through the setback.

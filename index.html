ADICT MANLUNG
Rapper on a mission

📍 Karungu, Kenya | Independent Hip-Hop

Adict Manlung is a raw, unfiltered voice from the streets of Karungu, Kenya. His music cuts through the noise with gritty realism, painting vivid pictures of struggle, ambition, poverty, survival, loyalty, and personal growth. Describing himself as a "rapper on a mission", Manlung's lyrical content draws directly from lived experiences in the trenches — the shanty compounds, the unforgiving blocks, and the unbreakable bonds formed in marginalized communities.

Since emerging in 2025, Manlung has released powerful tracks including My Gee, Unfinished Business, Black Africa, and Still Outside. His music serves as both social commentary and a beacon of resilience, shining light on systemic corruption, street loyalty, and the relentless pursuit of a better future. Each track is a chapter of his ongoing story — a piece of "unfinished business" with the world.

With a distinctive flow that blends classic hip-hop grit with Kenyan street pulse, Adict Manlung is building a legacy brick by brick. His upcoming release "Money Bag" promises to be his most ambitious statement yet.

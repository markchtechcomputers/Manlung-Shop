Who is Adict Manlung?

Adict Manlung is an independent Kenyan hip-hop artist and storyteller, based between Nairobi and Eldoret. He doesn't just make songs — he turns street realities into narrative: struggle, ambition, friendship, betrayal, survival and growth, told in order, like chapters.

He built the Flock without a label — writing, releasing and running the brand himself, and turning that independence into a wider world: music, streetwear, media and tech, all under one roof.

The storyteller

His records read as chapters, not singles — struggle leads to consequence, consequence leads to ambition. It's a more autobiographical, cinematic approach than the usual party-or-flex record.

Kenyan, on purpose

Nairobi and Eldoret aren't backstory — they're the perspective. The brand is built for Kenya, East Africa and the diaspora rather than sanded down for anywhere.

Independent by design

No traditional label. Adict Manlung writes, releases and runs the business side himself — artist and publisher in one.

12 Flock

The streetwear line that lets the Flock wear the story, not just stream it — previewed through the Flock Show, with a collaboration alongside Tashlie Brands.

Beyond the mic

Also an entrepreneur and cybersecurity engineer — the same independent, build-it-yourself instinct shows up in the tech and business side of Manlung.

About Adict Manlung

Adict Manlung is a Kenyan hip-hop artist known for raw, unfiltered storytelling — turning everyday struggle, street life, and ambition into music that hits close to home. Independent from day one, every release is self-driven: written, recorded, and put out without a label behind it.

The sound draws from classic boom-bap roots fused with a modern Nairobi edge, built for listeners who want lyrics with substance over throwaway hooks. Every single, CD, and piece of merch sold here goes straight back into the next project.

Beyond the music, Adict Manlung has stepped into fashion — featured at Flock Show and collaborating with Tashlie Brands, bringing the same street-rooted identity to style as to sound.

FASHION & MUSIC · ADICT MANLUNG

Beyond the Mic: Adict Manlung's Move Into Streetwear

Manlung Shop is a multi-platform digital brand founded by Adict Manlung, combining music, official merchandise, technology services, and cybersecurity resources under one trusted network. It is built to empower audiences with creative products, practical digital solutions, and a strong commitment to safe, ethical, and responsible technology use.

Adict Manlung has never just been about the music. Alongside building a catalog of raw, street-rooted hip-hop, he's been quietly building a presence in Kenyan fashion — and it's starting to show.

His own clothing line, 12 Flock, carries the same identity as his music: unfiltered, street-first, built for people who wear their story rather than hide it. It's not a side project bolted onto the brand — it's an extension of the same worldview that shows up in tracks like "Black Africa" and "Money Bag." The Money Bag Hoodie in the Merch section is the first piece to come out of that world, with more planned as the line grows.

On the footwear side, Adict Manlung has partnered with Tashlie on a shoe collection that leans into the same street-luxury lane — pieces designed to move between a studio session and a night out without missing a beat. The collaboration was previewed at Flock Show, where the collection got its first real audience reaction, and it's part of why "Certified Drip Influencer" isn't just a tagline on this site — it's earned.

Expect both the 12 Flock line and the Tashlie shoe collection to expand alongside new music drops — this shop will carry each piece as it releases, so keep an eye on Merchandise for what's next.

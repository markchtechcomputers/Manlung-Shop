About Adict Manlung
The Flock Storyteller / Kenya

More than
music.

Adict Manlung is a Kenyan hip-hop artist and storyteller whose music turns real-life pressure, street realities, ambition, friendship, betrayal, survival and growth into chapters. His sound draws from hip-hop, street poetry and conscious rap, while his independent approach keeps the writing, releases and creative direction close to the artist.
01 / THE ARTIST

Story before noise

His records are written as lived stories rather than disconnected singles — raw, cinematic and focused on lyrics that carry weight.

02 / THE SOUND

Street-rooted rap

Hip-hop, street poetry and conscious rap meet a modern Kenyan edge, giving the music a direct and personal voice.

03 / THE FLOCK

Built independently

The Flock extends beyond songs into culture, merchandise and a growing creative brand built around the same story.

Music Journey

The road
so far.

From personal experiences and street poetry to a growing independent catalogue, Adict Manlung is building a body of work where every release adds another chapter.

CHAPTER 01

Finding the voice

Life, struggle and observation became the raw material for a sound grounded in truth and street perspective.

CHAPTER 02

Building the catalogue

Records such as My Gee, Unfinished Business and Black African helped shape the identity of the storyteller.

CHAPTER 03

Still Outside

The journey continues with new releases that keep the focus on resilience, ambition and the people around the story.

CHAPTER 04

The Flock grows

Music now sits inside a wider movement of brand, fashion, media and direct connection with the audience.

Where he is now

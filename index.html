About

Adict Manlung is a Kenyan rapper and songwriter known for blending authentic storytelling with hard-hitting rap and drill music. His music reflects personal experiences, street life, ambition, resilience, and the realities faced by young people striving for success. Through vivid lyrics and confident delivery, he aims to inspire listeners while staying true to his journey.
